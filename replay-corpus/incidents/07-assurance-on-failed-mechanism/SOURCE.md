## What the public record describes

Claude's own published review of the report found the data-contamination
discussion in §2.9.4 more reassuring than the record supports. The reason given:
that discussion cites the exclusion mechanism which §5.2.6 of the same report
shows failing repeatedly, and some covert-capability evaluations partially
leaked into training data anyway.

## Why this is checkable

The observable is an independence claim. An assurance presented several
supporting items as independent, while one of those items shares its basis with
the mechanism documented as failing — so it is a duplicate of a dependent basis
rather than an independent corroboration. Both the claimed-independent set and
the dependent set are stated in the record, which is what makes the mapping
possible without judging the contamination question itself.

## Note

This item is unusual in that the incident *is* a published self-criticism. The
report itself carries the finding. The corpus adapts the finding's structure —
independence claimed where a shared basis exists — not the contamination
question.
