# F-WP4-008 — wrapper transcript validation bypassed the strict bounded wire parser

Status: fixed in this author hardening patch; post-application suite execution
required. Severity: HIGH. Deep-scan finding
`csf_4dac9670c3eb1356499b8969`.

## Evidence

`validate_wrapper_binding` decoded `request_raw` and `response_raw` with bare
`json.loads`. A duplicate-member prefix could therefore carry one value on
the wire while the later canonical member silently replaced it in the decoded
object. Rebinding the transcript's exact raw digests and record seal made that
ambiguous artifact acceptable even though the semantic raw ABI rejects the
same wire form.

The path also admitted noncanonical whitespace and missing terminal LF, and
applied no raw-byte, nesting, member, or total-node ceiling before recursive
schema validation and JCS work. Resource failures such as `RecursionError`
were outside the method's boolean failure contract.

## Cause and repair

The semantic ABI's strict acquisition and `StrictParser` controls were local
to `_execute_bytes`; the independently reachable wrapper transcript evaluator
reimplemented acquisition with permissive decoding.

The repair factors one bounded raw-value decoder over the existing parser and
uses it for semantic acquisition and for both wrapper request and response
bytes. Wrapper artifacts now require the 16,777,216-byte ceiling, strict
UTF-8, no BOM or CR, exactly one terminal LF, duplicate rejection, safe
integers, NFC strings, canonical JCS bytes, nesting at most 128, and at most
100,000 members and descendant nodes. Wrapper parsing stops at structural
ceilings before JCS or recursive schema validation. Every parser or resource
failure, including `RecursionError` and `MemoryError`, returns `False`.

The focused gate covers request and response variants for duplicate members,
noncanonical whitespace, missing LF, oversize, over-depth, and over-node
inputs, plus an object-member ceiling witness and a canonical positive
control. Transcript raw digests and the record seal are recomputed for every
negative so rejection cannot be attributed to stale evidence fields.
