# F-WP4-010 — raw structural and error-pointer bounds were enforced after amplification

Status: fixed in hardening wave W4. Deep-scan findings
`csf_5c9d8897a5f9b614a4920a6f`,
`csf_c4ae252fe3d0ab6247208fc1`,
`csf_4737a679e55194e03457cbfa`, and the second-implementation half of
`csf_ef91471c8515560f67b2ca97`.

## Evidence

The semantic raw path instantiated complete container frames until the wire
ended because its parser ran with structural enforcement disabled. Depth,
member, and node ceilings were consulted only after canonicalization and
schema work. Invalid-number and NFC scans also retained every fully rendered
pointer. The CLI performed an unbounded stdin read before the raw byte ceiling,
and error builders could serialize attacker-controlled pointers longer than
the response schema's 240-character maximum.

## Repair

The parser now enforces depth, member, and node ceilings during construction.
On the semantic ABI, a fixed-state close/truncation classifier preserves the
suite-pinned schema-before-limit results for closed over-depth root shapes
without rebuilding their trees; no over-limit value is admitted. Number and
NFC validation retain only the UTF-8-minimum pointer. The CLI reads at most
`MAX_RAW_BYTES + 1`, and both response builders convert an unrepresentable
pointer to deterministic root `ERR_LIMIT` before sealing.

The exact-pointer law and the response schema's 240-character maximum remain
mutually unsatisfiable for some inputs. W4 resolves that contradiction
fail-closed: exact pointers are preserved through 240 characters; longer
pointers are never truncated or emitted and instead produce `ERR_LIMIT`.
