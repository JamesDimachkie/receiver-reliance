## Reference law (pinned by 55 black-box probes; no reference source read)

- Gates, in order: root non-dict → `""`; missing or wrong top
  `format_version` → `/format_version`; missing `operation_handle`,
  `obligation_id`, or `decision_input` → `""`; non-dict `decision_input` →
  `""`.
- After the gates every mismatch pools and the UTF-8-minimum pointer is
  selected. No stage short-circuits an earlier pointer out of the pool, and
  no suppression is applied.
- Inside `decision_input`: a missing binding-owned member
  (`operation_handle`, `obligation_id`) contributes its member pointer; a
  missing or malformed structure-owned member (`format_version`, `facts`),
  an unknown member, or any arm failure contributes `/decision_input`.
- Member VALUES bound across copies (`operation_handle`, `obligation_id`,
  `request_id`, the two digests) belong exclusively to the binding stage at
  member pointers; structure is therefore judged on a canonical-value-repaired
  copy, and the canonical operation derives from however many of the three
  bound copies are present. Binding checks evaluate only when the request is
  structurally complete and each check's own members are present.
- Empty input: `ERR_EMPTY_INPUT` for zero bytes or exactly one LF.
