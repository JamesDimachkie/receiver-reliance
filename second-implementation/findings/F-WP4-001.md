## Cause and repair

The raw parser and JCS serializer recursively descended containers, coupling
wire totality to CPython's call-stack limit. Both were replaced with explicit
iterative stacks; Unicode traversal was made iterative too. `execute_bytes`
now contains unexpected exceptions as sealed `ERR_INTERNAL` responses so no
candidate API/CLI traceback can escape.

The gate covers open, balanced, object, array, and mixed structures at depths
496, 497, 498, 499, and 600 on both candidate surfaces. The bounded black-box
preflight compares the same 25 inputs across frozen reference, candidate API,
and candidate CLI.
