# F-WP5-008 — seven receipt provenance pins went stale and the verifier failed undisclosed

Status: **DISPOSED, NOT REBOUND.** The pins stay as the receipts publish them;
`ERRATA.md` E14 carries the disposition and
`perf/sidecar/verify_receipts.py` now enforces it, so the command reports
`checks=133 failures=0` instead of `checks=126 failures=7`. Raised by the
2026-08-18 third-party reproduction run, which found the command red at tip.

## What was wrong

Both admitted WP5 receipts carry a `source_sha256` map recording the bytes that
produced their profiling run. The hardening campaign then changed four of those
sources, so seven pin rows across the two receipts stopped matching:

| Source | Pinned at run | Current | Moved by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `grounded-0_4/rr_api.py` | `7774AA7B…` | `EE7B66F8…` | W1 bare-`decide` withdrawal; W3 runtime byte-authentication |
| `grounded-0_4/authority_surface.py` | `44BA458B…` | `62B689D9…` | W3 register nesting and vocabulary authentication |
| `grounded-0_4/rr_batch.py` | `BF38779E…` | `B271C6DB…` | W3 batch overlimit cap and OBL-30 R1–R3 pool bindings |
| `perf/sidecar/profile_robustness.py` | `1DD4E53E…` | `71B0CD88…` | the W3-adapters/W4 evidence rebind |

Four rows in `profile-windows-…-attempt7.json`, three in
`sidecar-parity-windows-…-attempt10.json`. Every other pin in both receipts —
sixteen and eighteen respectively, including all frozen contract, fixture and
engine bytes — still matches exactly.

The verifier exited 1 the whole time. Nothing surfaced it: `perf/SIDECAR.md`
listed the command under `## Verification` with no caveat, and `portable/gate.py`
carried the same failure as `sidecar-receipts exit=1`, leaving that gate at
`checks=9 failures=1`. So a third gate was red at tip alongside the charter gate
of E13, for the same reason in a different place — a red result that no summary
surfaced.

## Why the pins are not rebound

Two independent reasons, and either alone settles it.

The pins record which bytes produced a recorded run. Rewriting them to the
hardened digests would assert that the hardened sources produced the 2026-08-12
profiling numbers. They did not, and no run at current bytes has been recorded.

They also cannot be rewritten without breaking custody. The pins live inside the
receipt bodies, whose raw digests are pinned by `ADMITTED` in the verifier, by
the 60-file portable manifest, and by each receipt's own self-zero seal. Editing
a pin invalidates all three.

## Enforcement

`SOURCE_PIN_ERRATA` in `perf/sidecar/verify_receipts.py` maps each
(receipt, source) pair to the digest the receipt pins and the digest of the
current bytes. Each drifted row now produces two checks instead of one: the
erratum must quote the digest the receipt still publishes, so the historical pin
cannot be quietly rewritten; and the current bytes must equal the recorded
post-erratum digest, so a further undisclosed move of any of the four files
fails. Undrifted rows keep the exact-match check unchanged.

`perf/SIDECAR.md` now states the command's expected output and its scope: it
verifies recorded evidence, not current behaviour.

Same class as F-CONC-004 (ERRATA E12) and E13. All three are pins or transcripts
that stopped describing what they name, and all three were invisible because
what sat over them replayed recorded evidence rather than recomputing it.
`portability/verify_live.py` is the general answer; this finding is one of the
instances that motivated it.
