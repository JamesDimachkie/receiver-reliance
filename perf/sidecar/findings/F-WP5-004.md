# F-WP5-004

> **Correction (2026-08-12, F-WP5-007):** the claim below that the reader
> "captures every stderr byte" was false on the attempt-8 bytes — it
> returned after the first 64 KiB chunk. The reader now drains every byte
> with bounded 1 MiB retention, and `stderr_evidence()` discloses
> `total_bytes` and `sha256_scope`. — stderr validation stripped evidence

## Finding

Shutdown used stripped decoded stderr text. Whitespace-only stderr could pass,
and a bounded tail could omit the beginning of a long diagnostic stream.

## Correction

A dedicated reader captures every stderr byte and marks the first nonempty
chunk as a protocol failure. Decisions use raw byte count, never stripped text.
Evidence retains total bytes, exact SHA-256, and bounded prefix/tail hex.

## Regression evidence

One child emits three whitespace bytes (`20 0A 09`); another emits 5,000 `P`
bytes plus `TAIL`. Both are rejected and stopped. The receipt pins lengths,
digests, and both edges of the long stream.
