# F-WP1-002 — Alias conflict and freshness were bypassable

Historical general-adapter finding. F-WP1-009 later stood that surface down;
the fallback detects alias contradictions but does not claim mutable-state
freshness custody.

Status: repaired in code.

The first adapter preferred `claimed_path` over a conflicting
`referenced_record`, and a caller could reuse a profile after state mutation.

Repair: conflicting aliases refuse H1. Preflight requires an atomic
observer/revision callback; the revision-only route is removed. Capabilities
recheck the full record digest and revision at request build.
Regressions: `Finding002FreshnessAndAliasTests`.
