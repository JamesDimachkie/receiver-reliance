# F-WP1-009 — General host integration crossed the portable evidence boundary

Status: third same-class strike; general adapter stood down; named fallback
active.

The general host path failed three times on the same boundary class: host-only
semantics were treated as portable facts. Attempt 1 inferred acknowledgment
from event count/position. Attempt 2 distinguished typed evidence but collapsed
detectable contradictions and unavailable semantics into one refusal. Attempt
3 retained general adapter, transcript, replay, effect, and accepted-core
runner machinery whose correctness depended on mutable process-local state.
Those experiments cannot establish a transferable host contract.

Repair is stand-down, not another generalization attempt. `adapters.__init__`
exports only `RR-PORTABLE-PREFLIGHT-1`: native-evidence and optional
host-produced fact-profile validation with exactly `READY`,
`REJECTED_INVALID`, and `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`. Contradictions are evaluated
before applicability. The general adapter and H2/H5/H6 experiments remain only
as explicitly non-shipping measurement/regression code, are unreachable from
the exported API, and their direct CLI is disabled.

The receipt may use the pinned mutable runner after a `READY` result only to
score the recorded corpus. It declares that runner
`NON_SHIPPING_MEASUREMENT_ONLY`, `exported_from_adapters: false`, and
`claimable_as_fallback_capability: false`. Regressions:
`PortabilityAndBoundaryTests`, `OutcomeReceiptTests`, and
`Finding009FallbackBoundaryTests`.
